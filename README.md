# Minji's iPhone 📱

An interactive iPhone simulation with a playable KakaoTalk-style chat story: you are **Kim Minji** (NewJeans), a 2nd-year high-school student with a crush on her senior **Kim Juhoon** (CORTIS) — lead vocalist of the school band club and a basketball player. You meet through the school festival committee, and the story unfolds over chats, notes, photos, music, maps, and a final phone call. Fully written in Indonesian.

**▶ Live demo: https://minji-juhoon.vercel.app**

## How to play

1. Open the site and swipe up on the lock screen.
2. Enter the passcode **1121**.
3. Open **KakaoTalk** and read the chat with Juhoon — stay on the page: he messages you live (~40 seconds in), and the story builds toward his call (around the 4- and 6.5-minute marks).
4. Explore the other apps too: Notes (Minji's diary), Photos, Instagram (@juhoon), Music, Maps, Calendar, Mail, and Settings.

## Tech

Pure static HTML/CSS/JS — no build step, no dependencies. Just serve this folder with any static file server (e.g. `npx serve .`) or deploy it as-is to Vercel/Netlify/GitHub Pages.
